## Decision

**Source:** the roster `id`, its Kind (`person` or `publication`), and its name, with a reference-style link.

**Decision:** admit, watch-list, decline, promote, or demote.

**Marking or usage limit:** `**Corroborate.**`, `**Discovery-only.**`, an independence limit, or none.

**Other ecosystems:** the `id` and Kind it already has on another awesome-humans roster, or "none".

## Evidence per admission criterion

The criteria are in the repository's `AWESOME-HUMANS.md`: Admission criteria. Link the posts each finding rests on, as reference-style links.

- **Longevity:** the finding, and its evidence.
- **Depth:** the finding, and its evidence.
- **Accuracy:** the finding, and its evidence.
- **Independence:** the finding, and its evidence.
- **Dormancy:** the date of the latest published writing, and where it continues if the original channel went quiet.
- **Conflicts of interest:** the product the source sells that an opinion might recommend, or "none".

## Decision log line

The row added to the decision log: the decision and the fact behind it, in two sentences.

## Demotion

A demotion is finished only when no opinion cites the source. For each claim that cited it, the file, and whether it was re-sourced, from which roster ID, or dropped.

## Checked

That the roster row and the `## Source notes` section are in ID order, and that `just check` passes, with the sources check accepting every `sources:` list.
